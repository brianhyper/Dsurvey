Plant Stimulant Use Among Pharmacy Students

A confidential, self-administered research questionnaire on the prevalence and patterns of plant-based stimulant use (coffee, tea, energy drinks, khat, ginseng, kola nut, Leonotis leonurus) among pharmacy students at KMTC Nakuru. It takes about 10–15 minutes to complete.
